HOW THIS TOWN WORKS ✕
  • The goal: Bank the most policy victories by Election Night, November 2028 (22 months). A victory is credited to the single top contributor on a fight's winning side — the leaderboard in HQ REPORT tracks the race.
  • The loop: Scholars produce ✦ influence each month. Spend influence on policy fights and on courting donors. Donors pay the bills so you can hire more scholars. Victories cost influence the machine never gets back — that tension is the game.
  • Support: Ops staffers support 1–3 scholars each (shown on their card), and some carry traits — rainmakers, connectors, media bookers, or expensive tastes. Unsupported scholars (newest hires lose coverage first) produce at half rate and quit after 2 straight unsupported months. Beware 🔥 divas: enormous output, but colleagues keep quitting over them.
  • Raids: Candidates marked AT [RIVAL] currently work for the competition. Hiring one costs a 1.5× signing bonus and permanently dents that rival's monthly influence budget.
  • Fights: Commit influence to a side. When the clock hits zero, the wire decides: your side's share of influence sets its win odds (sharpened — a 2:1 lead wins ~85%, but upsets happen), shown live on every card. If your side wins, you get the reward scaled by your share of the winning side's total — cash, clout (✦), and sometimes a free scholar, a warm donor intro, or donor amnesty. Rivals commit every month — the ⚑ line under each side shows exactly who's backing it.
  • Expertise: Every scholar whose tag matches a fight amplifies influence you commit there by +10% (max +50%) — shown as the ★ chip on the card.
  • Partisan fit: Scholars who share your institution's politics sign for 50% less; aisle-crossers cost +50%. Simpatico donors court 30% cheaper; opposed ones +40%. Hardline shops (◀◀ / ▶▶) feel both effects harder: scholars 60% off / +75%, donors 40% off / +60%. The ▼/▲ tips on market cards show the exact before → after price.
  • Donors: Courting costs influence; grants arrive monthly. Every donor has a demand — a scholar on the roster, a vanity program, monthly influence pushed into their pet issue, or ideological purity (backing a side they hate = instant strike). Unmet demand = 1 strike per month. Two strikes and they walk. Grants run on cycles — when one ends you get a month to renew on stricter terms (half courting cost, tougher demand, one strike ends it) or let them lapse. Stale market? Prospecting buttons re-deal the hiring or donor market — for cash and influence, and each sweep of a market gets 50% pricier than the last.
  • Crises: Now and then the Bugle prints an EXTRA — a scandal, a funder feud, a shutdown — and you must pick your poison before the next month can begin. Every option costs something different.
  • Election season: The final six months are announced — rival spending runs 50% hotter until the vote. The endgame is the hardest part.
  • Payroll creep: Every December, everyone gets a raise (+9%) — and rivals occasionally make a run at your best scholars: match the offer (permanent raise) or let them defect and strengthen that rival. Standing still is a slow way to lose.
  • Programs: Money sinks (gala, podcast, lobby). Mostly useless for policy — but certain donors won't fund you without them. That's the game.
  • Losing: End two consecutive months with a negative treasury and the institution folds before the election. Otherwise, Election Night ranks everyone by victories banked.
