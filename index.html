The Beltway Bugle
HOW THIS TOWN WORKS ✕
  • The loop: Scholars produce ✦ influence each month. Spend influence on policy fights and on courting donors. Donors pay the bills so you can hire more scholars.
  • Support: Each ops staffer supports 2 scholars. Unsupported scholars (bottom of the roster) produce at half rate.
  • Fights: Commit influence to a side. When the clock hits zero, the bigger pile wins. If your side wins, you get the reward scaled by your share of the winning side's total. Rivals commit every month — the ⚑ line under each side shows exactly who's backing it.
  • Donors: Courting costs influence; grants arrive monthly. Every donor has a demand. Unmet demand = 1 strike per month. Backing positions an ideological donor hates = instant strike. Two strikes and they walk.
  • Programs: Money sinks (gala, podcast, lobby). Mostly useless for policy — but certain donors won't fund you without them. That's the game.
  • Losing: End two consecutive months with a negative treasury and the institution folds.
